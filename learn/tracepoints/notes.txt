common_* fields: Provided by the tracepoint infrastructure itself. These exist for all tracepoints, for example:
common_type, common_flags, common_preempt_count, common_pid
Used internally by ftrace and BPF to identify and manage events.
__syscall_nr: Which syscall is being invoked.
The actual syscall arguments: dfd, filename, flags, mode.
So args in your BPF program (ctx->args) are just the syscall-specific fields, not the common_* fields.

ctx->args is an array of the syscall arguments only, extracted by the BPF tracepoint helper.
It does not include common_* fields; those are part of the tracepoint struct in /sys/kernel/debug/tracing, but BPF gives you just the syscall args in ctx->args[].
So the mapping is:
/sys/kernel/debug/tracing field	ctx->args[] index
dfd	args[0]
filename	args[1]
flags	args[2]
mode	args[3]
Similarly, for close:
Field	args[] index
fd	args[0]
